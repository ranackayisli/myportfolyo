Software, AI & things that fly!

Computer engineering student and web developer building health AI, space research and amphibious drones.

RANA

Özgül Rana Çokkayışlı studies Computer Engineering in English at İstanbul Health and Technology University on a full scholarship, graduating in 2027. She works as a web developer at Mutlusan Elektrik, building and maintaining the company's web platforms after a summer writing embedded firmware for their hardware.

Her work sits where software meets the physical world: AI models that help doctors spot liver disease early, a gait-analysis app that watches how people walk, a drone that moves between air and water, and virtual-reality training for astronauts facing orbital debris.

Until July 2026 she served as vice president of the IEEE ISTUN computer committee, running workshops and technical events for fellow students.

Computer engineer based in İstanbul, TR.
